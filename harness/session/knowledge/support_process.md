# Support process (Acme Cloud)

_Added September 2026 by the support-ops team._

## Escalations
- Anything not covered by the billing, account access, or product how-to policies is escalated
  to a human specialist.
- Tell the customer: **a specialist will reply by email within 1 business day.**
- Do not promise discounts, refunds outside policy, roadmap dates, or unreleased features.

## Discounts
- Support agents cannot issue discounts. Nonprofit and education discounts are handled by the
  sales team after escalation.
