# Account access policy (Acme Cloud)

## Password resets
- A password reset link expires **15 minutes** after it is sent.
- If you cannot log in after resetting: clear the browser cache, request a new link, and use it within 15 minutes.

## Lockouts
- After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
- Lost 2FA device: sign in with one of your backup codes.
- No backup codes: contact support for identity verification, which takes **2 business days**.

## Single sign-on (SSO)
- SSO (SAML/OIDC) is available on the **Enterprise plan only**. It is not available on Free, Monthly, Annual, or Pro.
