# Billing policy (Acme Cloud)

_Last updated: July 1, 2026_

## Refunds
- Refunds are available within **14 days of purchase** for all plans (Monthly, Annual, Pro, Enterprise).
- This replaced the old 30-day window on July 1, 2026. Purchases made before July 1 keep the 30-day window.
- No prorated refunds are issued after the 14-day window.
- Request a refund at Settings → Billing → Request refund.

## Billing contact and invoices
- Change the billing email at Settings → Billing → Billing contact.
- Invoices are PDFs, downloadable from Settings → Billing → Invoices.

## Failed payments
- A failed payment is retried 3 times over 7 days.
- After the third failure the workspace is downgraded to the Free plan. No data is deleted.

## Currencies
- Acme Cloud bills in USD, EUR, and GBP.
